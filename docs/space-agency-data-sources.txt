
SRTM Global 1 Arc-Second DEM (GL1)
  Agency and mission: NASA / USGS / DLR - Shuttle Radar Topography Mission
  Resolution: 1 arc-second, about 30 m
  Access: NASA Earthdata HTTPS, tiles as .hgt
  In the repository: 10 tiles in data/tiles/ - N19W156, N29W014, N35W116,
    N36W117, N37E094, N37W007, N41W122, N43W114, S24W069, S24W070
  Feeds: the screening pass over five of those tiles (15,680 windows of 64 x 64
    cells, about 2 km each); measured ground for ten of the thirteen curated
    sites over a 601 x 601 cell window (about 18.5 km); the Atacama elevation,
    slope and roughness arrays behind the sample dossiers
  Derived outputs: reports/screened_candidates.json, reports/site_terrain.json,
    data/samples/srtm_earth_*.npy and srtm_earth_stats.json

LOLA Global Lunar DEM, 4 pixels per degree (LDEM_4)
  Agency and mission: NASA GSFC - Lunar Reconnaissance Orbiter / LOLA
  Resolution: about 7.58 km per pixel at the equator
  Access: PDS, imbrium.mit.edu (MIT)
  Feeds: the lunar south-pole reference - slope, roughness and elevation grids
    behind every Moon verdict, and the Moon side of all thirty engine reports
  Derived outputs: data/samples/lola_moon_south_pole_*.npy and
    lola_moon_stats.json

MOLA MEGaDR Global Topography, 4 pixels per degree (meg004)
  Agency and mission: NASA GSFC - Mars Global Surveyor / MOLA
  Resolution: about 14.8 km per pixel at the equator
  Access: PDS, pds-geosciences.wustl.edu; read through the native PDS3 reader
  Feeds: the Jezero reference - the Mars side of all thirty engine reports
  Derived outputs: data/samples/mola_mars_jezero_*.npy and mola_mars_stats.json

MGS TES Thermal Inertia Global Map
  Agency and mission: NASA / ASU / PSI - Mars Global Surveyor / TES
    (Putzig, Mellon and Boynton 2005)
  Resolution: about 3 km per pixel
  Access: PDS, se.psi.edu
  Status: PDS3 binary reader implemented and verified
  Feeds: the thermal comparison, which uses the map's published inertia ranges
    as a body-level envelope rather than reading per-pixel values

NASA GIBS (gibs.earthdata.nasa.gov)
  Draws the heat map, through a WMS request for MODIS_Terra_Land_Surface_Temp_Day
  at the site's bounding box, and the cloud layer on the globe, from VIIRS
  true-colour imagery on the same service.

NASA Images API (images.nasa.gov, pixels from images-assets.nasa.gov)
  Thirty-one agency frames across five of the thirteen sites, addressed by NASA
  asset id. Every URL was requested and checked before it was committed.

NASA Blue Marble and Earth at Night

  day and night faces.

NASA Deep Star Maps 2020 and the CGI Moon Kit
